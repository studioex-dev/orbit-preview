What every attendee holds

One card. No app.

The whole attendee layer is a single printed card. A QR three-word code on the front logs a connection in one scan; a four-digit PIN on the back recovers a session if a cookie is lost. Nothing to download, nothing to charge.

  • Scan One tap logs the connection for both people.
  • Three-word code Human-readable and typeable when a camera won't cooperate.
  • PIN Recovers a session with no login and no account.

No QR or scan system in place? We can build Orbit into your existing process, or simply print and send Orbit cards for your whole event.

  • 01

    Plugs into any registration or scan API you already use: Cvent, Swapcard, Grip, ExpoPlatform, in-house, anything.

  • 02

    No app for attendees to download. No new badge hardware. No extra queues at registration.

  • 03

    We never sit in front of your event. The attendee experience stays yours; Orbit works in the background on the data you already collect.

If your event captures scans, Orbit can read them. That's the entire integration.
